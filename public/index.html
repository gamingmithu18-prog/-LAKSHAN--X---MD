Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026Version 1.0Controller: Lakshan

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information 𝐋𝐀𝐊𝐒𝐇𝐀𝐍 𝐗 𝐌𝐃 collects, why it is collected, how it is stored and what rights you have over it. It applies to the pairing website and to the WhatsApp bot service.

2. Information We Collect

Session credentials. When you link your device, WhatsApp generates encrypted authentication credentials. These are stored in a private MongoDB database and are required to keep your bot connected. They are never shared with any third party.

Account identifiers. Your WhatsApp identifier, phone number in identifier form, and public display name are stored to associate settings and preferences with your session.

Usage counters. The name of each command you run and a running total of commands used are recorded to power statistics, leaderboards and abuse detection.

Group configuration. For groups where the bot is active, group identifiers, group names and feature toggles such as anti link or welcome settings are stored.

Technical logs. Temporary connection logs including timestamps and error messages may be produced by the hosting platform.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not read, archive or analyse the general content of your conversations.
  • We do not store your WhatsApp password, because WhatsApp never provides one to linked devices.
  • We do not collect payment information, since the service is free.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, third party analytics scripts or marketing cookies on this website.

4. Anti Delete Feature

When the anti delete feature is enabled, incoming messages are cached temporarily so that a deleted message can be recovered and forwarded to the bot owner inbox. These cached records are automatically and permanently purged after twenty four hours by a database expiry rule. You may disable this feature at any time with the corresponding command.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing is carried out on the basis of your explicit consent, given at the moment you voluntarily link your device. You may withdraw that consent instantly by unlinking the device in WhatsApp.

6. Data Storage and Security

All persistent data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas cluster protected by authentication, network restrictions and encryption in transit. Access is limited to the operator. While strong safeguards are applied, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be one hundred percent secure.

7. Data Retention

  • Session credentials are retained while your device remains linked, and are deleted when you log out or when WhatsApp invalidates the session.
  • Anti delete cache entries expire automatically after twenty four hours.
  • User and group records are retained until deletion is requested or the session is removed.

8. Third Party Services

Certain commands forward your query text to third party interfaces in order to return a result, for example media resolvers, movie index sites, translation services, weather services and artificial intelligence providers. Only the minimum query necessary is transmitted. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies, which are outside the operator control.

9. Your Rights

  • Access. You may request a copy of the data held about your session.
  • Erasure. You may request complete deletion of your session and associated records.
  • Withdrawal. You may unlink the device at any time, which immediately halts all further processing.
  • Correction. You may request that inaccurate stored information be corrected.

Requests can be made through the official support channel.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children under the age of thirteen. If it is discovered that data belonging to such a child has been collected, it will be deleted promptly upon notification.

11. Website Data

This pairing website stores no cookies for tracking purposes. A temporary session identifier is kept in browser memory only for the duration of the pairing process and is discarded when the page is closed.
